## Implementation and authority

The pure content domain owns normalized candidate uniqueness, one profile/candidate and one candidate/eligible-round limits, the ten-profile threshold, moderation eligibility, ten lifetime wins, exactly three proposed pairs, one open suggestion, reasoned independent review, one proposer appeal, and distinct Senior Moderator uphold/overturn. The server verifies a current revealed no-hint solve and current player membership under room/match locks. Stable profile wins and fresh moderator roles come from private server-owned records. Client-supplied roles/wins are rejected.

A bounded serialized content aggregate owns cross-room uniqueness. Case changes, relational nominations, canonical results, one outbox intent and audit rows commit together. The SQL projection applies domain-computed eligibility rather than reimplementing moderation policy. A deliberate outbox failure proves rollback preserves the prior case, votes and committed audits. Canonical retries precede current membership/role checks. Public/other-player projections contain no voter identities or internal records; authorized reviewers receive decision history. The aggregate caps 512 cases and 4096 nominations and returns recoverable SERVICE_LIMIT at capacity rather than silently truncating or buying a service.

Approval and activation are separate commands. Only an authorized independent moderator can activate complete curated records with matching proposed targets, exactly three hints, explicit alternatives and one of the original repository backgrounds. Immutable pair versions preserve current and historical rounds. Exclusions affect future selection only. The catalog cache validates a database revision on every read, and match commands share-lock that revision before deciding; a stale draw conflicts. Initial startup requires the ten immutable seed pair IDs and at least one selectable pair in each initial category. Excluding the final selectable initial pair is rejected atomically: curate a replacement first. Reopening a rejected suggestion through appeal also checks the one-open-suggestion invariant and returns CONFLICT without audit/state/command-record changes.

Reactions use a separate room-anchored durable aggregate with fresh membership and active-round evidence. The only accepted values are 👍, ❤️, 😂, 🎉 and 👀. Each actor has an exact two-second server rate boundary, and reactions have absolute five-second expiry. Canonical retry does not extend expiry or send twice. No room/match version, submission, hint, score or category state is changed. Private Realtime notifications carry only aggregate identity/version; authorized clients refetch visible reactions. Outsiders receive no room notification or projection.

The game provides the fixed reaction menu and eligible no-hint nomination form. Community content provides lifetime-win eligibility, structured three-pair suggestions, own status/reasons, independent review, appeal, senior resolution, complete curation and future exclusion forms. Uncertain mutations retain their exact endpoint, payload and command ID for explicit retry. Slow polling is serialized/coalesced: a React/jsdom regression with 1.2-second responses verifies reactions remain visible, nomination becomes enabled, and at most one fetch runs at a time. Accepted-but-lost nomination results are recovered through the own-round eligibility projection.
