- [Public stopped-work handoff](handoff.md). Product development, deployments and the deleted timer remain stopped.

The raw private reviewer streams and full canonical histories are not suitable for public upload. Full structured historical verdicts and bounded extracts retain the substantive findings, scores, identity and original hashes without publishing private bootstrap/profile context. Numeric token metadata is repeated-context processing, not billions of unique words or a billing invoice. Missing provider usage remains unknown. Local paths in evidence identify original retained artifacts; use this package's manifest to find their public copies.
